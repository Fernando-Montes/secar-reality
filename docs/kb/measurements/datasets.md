**Resolved, 2026-08-07 (user, direct statement)**: the CSV files
(`_xf_cosy.csv`/`_yf_cosy.csv`) are **measured data**, not simulation
output — they are the X/Y projections obtained by processing the raw
viewer PNG images (summing/projecting pixel intensity along one axis).
So the `_cosy` suffix does not mean "generated by COSY" — most likely
"formatted/processed for comparison against COSY/ECAT output," per the
earlier first guess, though the exact reason for the name itself is
still not confirmed. What matters is now settled: these CSVs are the
programmatic form of the same measured data the user read manually off
the PNGs for the `E2` sheet analysis (see `runs.md`) — i.e. `E2`'s
values and these CSVs are two different extractions of the same
underlying measurement, not two different datasets.
Source: user, 2026-08-07 conversation
Recorded: 2026-08-07

`groupsInfo` (ViewerAnalysis.py:17-123) gives, per group, the
alignment/condition metadata used to set up the matching simulation
run:
- `target_alignment`: `{X, Y, R}` in meters — same for every group
  (`X=0.03055mm, Y=-0.01099mm, R=0.75mm`), so this is a fixed target
  spot definition, not a per-group variable.
- `pp_alignment`: `{X, Y, R, label}` in meters — pepperpot-mask
  alignment, **varies by group**. Labels encode two things: a pepperpot
  hole/angle setting (e.g. "1 MM (0 mrad Position)", "20 mrad (0 mrad
  Position)", "1 MM (+15 mrad Position)", "3 hole (+Y-position grp
  19)") and a beam energy-spread setting (e.g. "0% dE/E", "-2% dE/E").
- `fp1_slits`: `{slits_mm, label}` — FP1 slit acceptance window in mm,
  varies by group (e.g. `(-7,7)`, `(-21,-7)`, `(7,21)`, `(-50,50)`).

Groups 1-4 are "0% dE/E" at two pepperpot positions ("1 MM", i.e.
presumably a 1mm single-hole mask) with different FP1 slit windows.
Groups 5-9 add "20 mrad" pepperpot position and "-2% dE/E". Groups
10-17 are "1 MM" at "-2% dE/E" across several angular pepperpot
positions (0, +15, +7.5, -15, -7.2, +20, -20 mrad). Groups 18-19 use a
"3 hole" pepperpot mask at "-2% dE/E". Group 1 entry at
ViewerAnalysis.py:19-22 is a near-duplicate of group 2
(ViewerAnalysis.py:23-27) — not yet clear if that's intentional
(e.g. a repeat measurement) or a copy-paste artifact.
Source: ViewerAnalysis.py:17-123
Recorded: 2026-08-07

## Resolved 2026-08-07 (were open questions, see `runs.md` for the full methodology)
- **Authoritative dataset**: `PartD_ViewerAnalysis/Group{1..19}/`, via
  manually-curated readings of the per-viewer PNG figures (not the CSV
  files directly). `E3_optimization/`'s role is still unconfirmed.
- **Agreement metric**: not `chisq_1d` (that's used elsewhere in the
  code, e.g. `main_new2.py:146`, for a different workflow). For this
  work the figure of merit is **matching the slopes dX/dE and
  dX/dalphaX** (spot-position response to energy deviation and to
  incoming angle) between simulation and data at each viewer — *not*
  matching absolute mean spot position. dX = shift in mean spot
  position at a given viewer; dE and dalphaX are derived per-group from
  the experimental conditions (aperture position, collimator size,
  dE/E, FP1 slits) recorded in the sheet's summary columns.
- **Knobs in scope**: lattice/magnet `tuneChanges` (quadrupole `B_SC`
  scale factors, Wien filter E/B field strength, `TA_Y` misalignment),
  not just initial-distribution parameters — confirmed by the specific
  knobs used in the E2 sheet (Q6, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q11, WF1, WF2, Q12,
  Q13, Q14, TA_Y). See `runs.md`.

### Beam per group
**[VERIFIED]** `fp2_metrics.build_initial_distribution(group, nRays)`:
aperture type; target and pepperpot from `groupsInfo` (target R 0.75 mm;
pepperpot 0.5 mm for 1 MM groups, 10.46 mm for 20 mrad groups) at 0.48619 m;

Source: AgentSearch/fp2_metrics.py, fp3_metrics.py.

### FP2 loss (4-term, used to select the FP2 family)
**[VERIFIED]** sum of ((obs - target)/sigma)^2 over M12 -0.2472 (0.02),
Offset12 1.543 mm (0.2), M16 -0.3842 (0.03), Offset16 0.537 mm (1.5).
1-sigma acceptance (4 dof) 4.7196, 2-sigma 9.716. The FP2 OAT screen/ranking
used only the M12 and M16 terms (plus a constant Q6 prior).
Source: archive/2026-09-21/fp2_family_mc.py:107-111; fp2_oat_screen.py.

### FP3 loss (Mahalanobis on M12/Offset12)
**[VERIFIED]** d12^T C12^-1 d12 + ((M16 + 2.0956)/0.2)^2 +
((Offset16 - 3.0608)/1.5)^2, with C12 the covariance of the 3-point OLS fit to
the measured FP3 dalphaX data divided by 4 (user, 2026-08-18): sigma_M12 0.093,
sigma_Off12 0.98 mm, correlation +0.76; targets M12 -0.2382, Offset12 4.338 mm.
M16/Offset16 deliberately independent (fit correlation only -0.12).
Source: AgentSearch/fp3_loss.py (extracted unchanged from fp3_family_mc.py).

## E2 groups 5 and 7 (recorded 2026-10-24)
**[VERIFIED]** Fernando, 2026-10-24: treat groups 5 and 7 as the same setting measured twice (both dE 0.16% in
fp2_metrics/fp3_metrics), but they are not really the same. E2 used a thick target at the start of SECAR to create
energy and angular spread; the energy spread was wide and the intensity almost flat between the peak of the
energy distribution and dE/E = -2%. Group 5: SECAR tuned to the mean expected energy loss after the target
(0%). Group 7: SECAR tuned with a -2% energy offset. The simulation gives them identical rays (DigitalTwin
noticed, 2026-10-24); the data differ (FP2 measured X -0.57 vs +1.83 mm), so this difference is not modeled.

## FP2 Mahalanobis loss (decided by Fernando 2026-10-24; replaces the 4-term loss for new FP2 fits)
**[VERIFIED]** loss = rᵀ C⁻¹ r, r = [M12, Offset12, M16, Offset16] − targets (−0.2472, 1.543 mm, −0.3842, 0.537 mm).
C: diagonal σ = 0.027, 0.36 mm, 0.3, 1.5 mm (Fernando's choice; the fits give 0.0269, 0.3566, 0.2961, 0.425);
off-diagonal covariances kept as from the OLS fits to the measured FP2 data: cov(M12, Off12) = −0.00090
(corr −0.094, 5 groups), cov(M16, Off16) = −0.0084 (corr −0.067, 7 groups); no /4 scaling. Blocks M12/Off12 and
M16/Off16 independent. (Interpretation of "covariance terms stay the same" recorded by secar-reality.)
Fit knobs (2026-10-24): Q1–Q7, H1, H2, O1 fixed at the E2 Hall settings; candidates (a) B1–B4 exit dB, WF1 field,
H3; (c) Q1–Q7 X, Y, Roll, Yaw, Pitch (yaw/pitch as for FP3; misalign_blocks extended to Q1–Q7). Not (b) beam at
target: centroid verified on a viewer during E2, angle set by the pepperpot.
